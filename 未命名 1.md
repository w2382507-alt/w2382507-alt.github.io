# Evolution process

formation process of an SMBH out of an SIDM halos (5 stages)

1. **Initial state**: fiducial halo has a virial mass of Mvir = 107.5 M⊙ and forms at z = 15, when
sufficiently massive halos already exist but baryonic effects remain relatively weak. Initialized with a **NFW density profile**(c = 10)
2. **stage 1**: initial **core expansion**
3. **stage 2**: runaway **core collapse**. form a dense central core within a lower density envelope at z = 10.9
4. **stage 3**: the core constracts, losing mass until reaching the apparent-hotizon condition. **BH seed form** at z = 15, 30 M⊙
5. **Stage 4**: BH rapidly accretes the residual SIDM core. General-relativistic effects trigger a inflow to the BH, near free-fall. the BH mass rises from ~30 M⊙ to ~300 M⊙.
6. **Stage 5**: after the residual core is depleted, the BH begins to accrete the outer dark-matter envelope.SIDM heat conduction weakens central thermal pressure support, leads to  **super-Bondi Accretion**.
7. **final state**: gravitational capture overtakes gravothermal inflow, causing Bondi-like Accretion
