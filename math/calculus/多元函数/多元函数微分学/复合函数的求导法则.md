#定理 

若函数 $x=\varphi(s,t),y=\psi(s,t)$ 在点 $(s,t)\in D$ 可微，$z=f(x,y)$ 在点 $(x,y)$ 可微，则符合函数
$$z=f(\varphi(s,t),\psi(s,t))$$
在点 $(s,t)$ 可微，且它关于 s 与 t 的偏导数分别为
$$\dfrac{\partial z}{\partial s}\bigg|_{(s,t)}=\dfrac{\partial z}{\partial x}\bigg|_{(x,y)}\dfrac{\partial x}{\partial s}\bigg|_{(s,t)}+\dfrac{\partial z}{\partial y}\bigg|_{(x,y)}\dfrac{\partial y}{\partial s}\bigg|_{(s,t)}$$
$$\dfrac{\partial z}{\partial t}\bigg|_{(s,t)}=\dfrac{\partial z}{\partial x}\bigg|_{(x,y)}\dfrac{\partial x}{\partial t}\bigg|_{(s,t)}+\dfrac{\partial z}{\partial y}\bigg|_{(x,y)}\dfrac{\partial y}{\partial t}\bigg|_{(s,t)}$$

---
**证**

由 $x=\varphi(s,t),y=\psi(s,t)$ 在点 $(s,t)\in D$ 可微（[[多元函数的可微]]），于是
$$\Delta x=\dfrac{\partial x}{\partial s}\Delta s+\dfrac{\partial x}{\partial t}\Delta t+\alpha_{1}\Delta s+\beta_{1}\Delta t\tag{1}$$
$$\Delta y=\dfrac{\partial y}{\partial s}\Delta s+\dfrac{\partial y}{\partial t}\Delta t+\alpha_{2}\Delta s+\beta_{2}\Delta t\tag{2}$$
其中当 $\Delta s,\Delta t$ 趋于零时，$\alpha_{1},\alpha_{2},\beta_{1},\beta_{2}$ 都趋于零。又由 $z=f(x,y)$在点 (x,y) 可微（[[多元函数的可微]]），所以
$$\Delta z=\dfrac{\partial z}{\partial x}\Delta x+\dfrac{\partial z}{\partial y}\Delta y+\alpha\Delta s+\beta\Delta t\tag{3}$$
其中当 $\Delta x,\Delta y$ 趋于零时，$\alpha,\beta$ 都趋于零。将 (1)、(2)式代入 (3) 式，得
$$\Delta z=(\dfrac{\partial z}{\partial x}\dfrac{\partial x}{\partial s}+\dfrac{\partial z}{\partial y}\dfrac{\partial y}{\partial s})\Delta s+(\dfrac{\partial z}{\partial x}\dfrac{\partial x}{\partial t}+\dfrac{\partial z}{\partial y}\dfrac{\partial y}{\partial t})\Delta t+\bar{\alpha}\Delta s+\bar{\beta}\Delta t\tag{4}$$
其中
$$\bar{\alpha}=\frac{{\partial z}}{\partial x}\alpha_{1}+\frac{{\partial z}}{\partial y}\alpha_{2}+\frac{{\partial x}}{\partial s}\alpha+\frac{{\partial y}}{\partial s}\beta+\alpha \alpha_{1}+\beta \alpha_{2}$$
$$\bar{\beta}=\frac{{\partial z}}{\partial x}\beta_{1}+\frac{{\partial z}}{\partial y}\beta_{2}+\frac{{\partial x}}{\partial t}\alpha+\frac{{\partial y}}{\partial t}\beta+\alpha \beta_{1}+\beta \beta_{2}$$
由于  $x=\varphi(s,t),y=\psi(s,t)$ 在点 $(s,t)$ 可微，因此它们在点 $(s,t)$ 连续（[[多元函数可微的必要条件2#^6b79f0]]），即当$\Delta s,\Delta t \to 0$时，有$\Delta x,\Delta y \to 0$，从而$\alpha,\beta,\alpha_{1},\alpha_{2},\beta_{1},\beta_{2}$ 都趋于零。于是当 $\Delta s,\Delta t \to 0$ 时，$\bar{\alpha}\to 0,\bar{\beta}\to 0$，故由 (4) 式推的复合函数可微
