#定理

假设 $f = u + iv: G \to \mathbb{C}$为复变函数，f在$z_{0} \in G$处复可微的充要条件是：
1. $u, v$ 在 $z_{0} \in G$处可微
2. 满足Caucy-Rieman条件
$$\dfrac{\partial u}{\partial x} = \dfrac{\partial v}{\partial y},\dfrac{\partial u}{\partial y} = -\dfrac{\partial v}{\partial x}$$

且有
$$f'(z)=\dfrac{\partial u}{\partial x}+\dfrac{\partial v}{\partial x}i=\dfrac{\partial v}{\partial y}-\dfrac{\partial u}{\partial y}i$$

---
证明
已知f在$z_{0}$处复可微（[[复变函数可微#^cf624a]]）记$f'(z_{0}) = a+bi$，则有
$$f(z_{0}+h) - f(z_{0}) - (a+bi)(h_{1}+ih_{2}) = o(h)$$
其中$h_{1},h_{2}$分别为h的实部和虚部，由于[[复变函数极限存在充要条件#^73c3c1]]，u,v分别满足
$$u(z_{0}+h)-u(z_{0})-(ah_{1}-bh_{2})=o(h)$$
$$v(z_{0}+h)-v(z_{0})-(bh_{1}+ah_{2})=o(h)$$
这说明 u,v 在$z_{0}$处可微（[[多元函数的可微]]），且偏导数满足
$$\dfrac{\partial u}{\partial x} = \dfrac{\partial v}{\partial y},\dfrac{\partial u}{\partial y} = -\dfrac{\partial v}{\partial x}$$
