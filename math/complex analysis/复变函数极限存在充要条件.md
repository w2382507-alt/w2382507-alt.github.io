$$\lim_{ z \to z_{0} }=A \Leftrightarrow \lim_{ z \to z_{0} } u(z) = u_{0},\lim_{ z \to z_{0} } v(z)=v_{0}$$ ^73c3c1

证明
由于
$$\max\{|u(z)-u_{0}|,|v(z)-v_{0}|\}\leq|f(z)-A|\leq|u(z)-u_{0}|+|v(z)-v_{0}|$$
故
$$\lim_{ z \to z_{0} }=A \Leftrightarrow \lim_{ z \to z_{0} } u(z) = u_{0},\lim_{ z \to z_{0} } v(z)=v_{0}$$
